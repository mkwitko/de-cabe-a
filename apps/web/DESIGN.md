# Design — De Cabeça (web)

Derived from the shipped app (`src/App.tsx`, `src/index.css`, pages and games), updated 2026-11-06. Product truth lives in `/PRODUCT.md`; the surface direction contract in `.impeccable/surfaces/`.

## World

A printed sticker album. The page is pitch-green album paper with a fine halftone; games, levels and answers are **slots** that get a **sticker** pasted in. Empty = dashed slot with its printed number; filled = white-bordered paper sticker. Clubs are round colour badges with initials, never logos.

## Colour (Tailwind v4 `@theme`, oklch)

Strategy: Committed. Pitch green owns the page; album-cover yellow is the only accent.

| Token        | Value          | Use                                                    |
| ------------ | -------------- | ------------------------------------------------------ |
| `pitch`      | 0.22 0.045 155 | Page ground                                            |
| `pitch-deep` | 0.17 0.035 155 | Halftone dots, `html` ground, scrollbar track          |
| `chalk`      | 0.95 0.015 150 | Text on pitch                                          |
| `chalk-soft` | 0.80 0.04 150  | Secondary text, slot outlines and numbers (with alpha) |
| `paper`      | 0.93 0.008 100 | Sticker border                                         |
| `cover`      | 0.84 0.15 95   | Brand sticker field, selection, focus ring             |
| `ink`        | 0.27 0.07 152  | Text on paper/cover                                    |
| `stamp`      | 0.90 0.16 95   | "Em breve" rubber stamp                                |

One theme, dark (2026-11-10): no light values, no `prefers-color-scheme` or `data-theme` switching; `theme-color` `#0f1f16`.

## Type

One family, self-hosted: **Archivo Variable** (`@fontsource-variable/archivo/wdth.css`, wght 100–900, wdth 62–125%).

- Display/labels: `font-condensed` utility (`font-stretch: 68%`), weight 800–900, uppercase, tracking ≥ −0.02em. Brand: `clamp(3rem, 14vw, 5.5rem)`, leading 0.85, no wrap.
- Slot numbers: condensed 900, `text-7xl`, `tabular` (tabular numerals), `chalk-soft/50`.
- Body: normal width, 500–600, `text-lg`/`text-xl`, measure ≤ 34ch.

## Components

- **Brand sticker** — `paper` border 10px radius, `cover` field, rotated −2°, soft offset shadow.
- **Slot** — 2px dashed `chalk-soft/60` border, `chalk/6%` fill, radius 8px; number top-left, title + rule bottom. Game slots on the home show progress (`0/200 fases`, stars, bar) and the next action.
- **Stamp** — 2px `stamp` border, condensed uppercase, rotated −8°.
- **Track grid** (`TrackPage`) — progress panel (done/total, stars, "Jogar fase N"), then pages of 10 level slots; the open level is a `cover` sticker, done levels show their stars, locked ones stay dashed.
- **Career Path passport** (`CareerPathCard`, `games/career-path/Trail`) — burgundy leather cover with gold tooling and seal, the name in gold foil, a ruled visa page tucked in it (title in its frame and tone, last stamps and the lit next slot, progress, rarest sticker) and one `cover` button. Home: the cover fills the first phone screen; from `lg` a ticket beside it shows what the next level is worth. Trail: a spread of two visa pages, 6 stamp slots each (ink stamp + stars when done, lit dashed slot when next, faint when locked), turned 12 levels at a time.
- **Home scene, "Vestiário à noite"** (`components/LockerRoom.tsx`) — full-screen locker room (our own art, `public/img/vestiario-night.webp`, fixed and still); the passport lies open on the bench (inside cover: name, rule, title in its frame; visa page: 10 stamp slots, 5 on phones, and the count), the player's Career Path stickers fanned out of it, the next-level ticket clipped to it (stub with the level and what it is worth, one big ink button "Jogar fase N"), the closed modes as premiere posters taped to the wall under the "Em breve" stamp (one row that scrolls sideways with snap and faded edges, ~1.3 posters on a phone, 4–5 wide, arrows from `lg`): each the mode's art full-bleed under its own hue, halftone, folds, grain, a chipped corner and two strips of tape, the name in condensed 900 sized in container units, a one-line hook, a `stamp` "Em breve · Votar" strip, a round votes sticker (`cover` when it is the player's vote, "Seu voto") and a `cover` "Mais votado" ribbon on the leader; hover/focus lifts and straightens it; a tap opens `ModeDialog` (`?modo=`). The scene is static: no entry choreography, parallax, dust or light cone.
- **Trajectory** (Career Path) — vertical path of club badges joined by a dashed line; each club: name, country, years, games/goals in `cover`; the next unknown club is a dashed `?` badge.
- **Clue chip** — `cover` pill, small uppercase label + condensed value ("Posição · Meio-campista / Atacante"); main position first, then secondary ones.
- **Dugout panel** — the guess box (search + "Chutar" + skip) beside the board on wide screens, docked below it on phones.
- **Grid board** — 3×3 cells with criteria headers (club badge, flag or position); a right answer pastes a sticker into the cell; hints show a `HintMask` ("R______ _______").
- **Attempt dots** — dashed squares that fill per attempt, top-right of the game header.
- **Result sticker** (`LevelEnd`) — one pasted sticker (paper border, `cover` field, −1°, paste animation) with headline, detail, ink stars and points; then next/share buttons and the level's Top 10 (empty state "O lugar 1 está vago").
- **Title ladder** (Stats) — Reserva 0, Titular 15, Craque 60, Lenda 150 stars as a stepped track; the current title is a big cover sticker.
- **Player sticker** (Conta) — initials badge, nickname, title and stars on `pitch-deep`, paper border, −2°.
- **Buttons** — primary: `cover` fill, ink condensed uppercase, min-height 44px; secondary: 2px `chalk-soft/60` outline.

## Layout and motion

**Rule: use as much of the screen as possible, always centred.**

- One shell for every page: `max-w-7xl` (1280px, the product's widest target), `mx-auto`, gutters 16px (`px-4`) on phones and 32px (`sm:px-8`) from 640px. No page narrows the shell; there is no narrow/wide variant.
- `scrollbar-gutter: stable` on `html`, so the centre does not move between pages with and without a scrollbar.
- Width is filled by columns, not by stretching lines: the content regroups into 2 columns (`md`/`lg`) or a board + side panel, never a single narrow column floating to one side. Example: Conta puts the nickname and account forms side by side from `lg`; Stats puts Career Path and Grid side by side.
- Text keeps its measure (body ≤ 34–48ch) inside its column; the column, not the line, takes the width.
- Single elements without a column (empty states, the guest sticker on phones) are centred.
- Mobile-first (360px base): one column, no horizontal scroll at 360px.
- **Navigation** (updated 2026-11-09): four places, each owning its pages. Jogos (home, tracks, levels, Modo Carreira), Desafio (`/d/*`), Ranking (ranking, seasons, `/ligas`, `/liga/*`), Perfil (`/stats`, `/history`, `/conta`, `/perfil/*`). Phone: bottom bar 64px + safe area, four equal columns, 22px drawn icon over a 12px bold label, active tab in `cover` with a 3px cover bar on its top edge. Wide screens: the same four words in condensed uppercase at the top right, the active one underlined by the same bar. At launch the places are Jogar · Álbum · Perfil. Updated 2026-11-10: the header is a **scoreboard strip** (`.scoreboard`: black glass with a fine LED grid, a gold floodlight hairline on top, radius 16px, 56px tall) on every route: crest + name (the h1 on the home), places (wide only; the active one lit from the strip's floor with a `cover` bar and a soft cover wash, `.tab-lit`), one gear for **Ajustes**, Entrar/avatar. Phone: the same strip with crest, gear, Entrar on every route except inside a level (the game owns the phone screen); the bottom bar uses the same scoreboard material, the lit bar on the active tab's top edge. **Ajustes** (`components/Settings.tsx`, native `<dialog>`): a bottom sheet on phones, a 26rem panel under the strip's right end from 640px; Música and Efeitos switches with live volume faders (`.fader`), Trilha (Automática or one of four CC0 football loops, each with a 12 s preview), Vibração, Reduzir animações (`data-motion="reduce"` on `<html>`), Conta, Privacidade, cookies. Saved in `dc_settings` on the device.
- **Icons** (`src/components/Icons.tsx`): authored SVG on a 24px grid, 2px round stroke (nav, clock, trophy, chevron). Stars use `StarIcon`. No emoji or text glyphs as icons.
- **Headings carry their own name**: no small uppercase label above a heading (the home cards are titled "Desafio do Dia" and "Modo Carreira" themselves).
- Motion (performance first, decided 2026-11-10): the app must feel instant on a weak phone, so motion is cut to feedback only. Allowed: press states, sheet open/close, the route fade and short entrances (paste-in, stamp, fade), all ≤200 ms and `transform`/`opacity` only; the result reveal is one 150 ms step. Not allowed: anything that loops (sheen, shimmer, float, breathe, glow, dust), parallax or pointer/tilt tracking, `backdrop-filter`/blur, `mix-blend-mode`, animated `filter` or `box-shadow`, 3D transforms for decoration, Lottie. The album changes page with a 150 ms crossfade (swipe or tap); stickers, posters and the passport are still. Ajustes › Reduzir animações (and the system setting) ends every remaining transition and animation (`:root[data-motion='reduce']` and `prefers-reduced-motion` in `index.css`).

## Pitch and sound

- **Pitch behind the page** (`body::before`, fixed): mowing stripes (pitch-deep at 22%, 9vh bands) and field markings as an inline SVG of a vertical 68 × 105 pitch, `slice`-fitted (whole pitch on a phone, centre circle on wide screens), white at 7% opacity. The album halftone stays on top. Never strong enough to compete with text.
- **Sound** (`src/lib/sound.ts`): synthesised with Web Audio (no files): whistle when a level starts, rising chord + crowd on a right answer, falling "uhh" on a wrong one, cheer on a 3-star finish. **Off by default**; toggle in Perfil › Conta › Preferências (and in the top nav on wide screens), choice kept on the device.
- **Photos**: Wikimedia Commons only, always credited — under the photo in the card, as the thumbnail's title in the search list, and on `/creditos` (linked from the list).

## Browser surfaces

`::selection` cover on ink; `:focus-visible` 3px cover outline, 3px offset; `scrollbar-color` chalk-soft on pitch-deep; `color-scheme: dark`.

## Modo Clube career: the club is a place (2026-11-17)

Scope: `/tecnico/carreira` (`games/manager/career/ClubCareerPage.tsx`, styles under "sala" and "club-tunnel" in `index.css`). Owner direction, replacing the CSS 3D stadium of the same day.

- **World**: each destination of the career is one still, cinematic plate of a room in an old stadium at night: dark green enamel, worn wood, one amber lamp against cold floodlight. The interface is set into the room, never onto a flat ground.
- **Plates** (`public/img/clube/<room>-h.webp` landscape, `-v.webp` portrait, picked by `orientation`): `sala` (coach's office, the hub), `campo` (pitch side, the start screen), `tunel` (the walk before a watched match), `vestiario` (dressing room: squad and line-up; its landscape plate is the home's `vestiario-night.webp`). Our own art, FLUX 3 Klein on the owner's desktop, 1344×768 and 768×1344, 8 steps; prompts and seeds in `docs/pesquisa/modo-clube/arte/placas-clube.json`. No people, no logos. Small scribbles on boards and hoardings are not text and stay unreadable.
- **Scene** (`.sala-scene`): the plate is fixed behind the page, `object-fit: cover`, anchored top. Over it one static veil: the club's colour as light from the top (`--club-a`, 26%) and a dark fall toward the desk so data reads.
- **Lettering on the scene** (`.sala-glass`, `.sala-bar`): Archivo condensed 900 uppercase in chalk with a deep text shadow, set straight on the plate; the next match (both badges and names) sits over the office window. Gold (`club-gold`) is the only action colour.
- **Desk** (`.sala-desk`, `.sala-slab`): data lives on one dark slab (`oklch(0.15 0.014 160 / 0.94)`, radius 14px) lit by a gold hairline on its top edge and split by hairlines into panes (`.sala-pane`): table, last matches, next fixtures, messages, money. No separate cards, no nested panels.
- **Motion** (allowed here by the owner, beyond the app-wide 200 ms rule): one arrival per screen, `transform`/`opacity` only, nothing loops. The plate settles from 1.07 scale in 1.4 s; the two sides of the fixture rise once; the tunnel plate pushes toward the light (scale 1 to 1.16) while the match loads. `prefers-reduced-motion` ends all of it.
- **Shell** (2026-11-20, `.club-shell`): a running career owns the viewport; the site's scoreboard strip and bottom bar are hidden on `/tecnico/carreira` (`App.tsx` `stage`). From `lg` a fixed rail on the left (15rem, `.club-rail`) carries the crest, the club and division, the five rooms with a drawn icon each and the way out; the plate starts where the rail ends. On the phone the rooms are a five-tab bottom bar with short names. A sticky bar (`.club-top`) holds the room's name (the club and the date on the phone), the date, the cash and, in every room but the office, one gold button back to the match (`.club-go`, desktop only: on the phone the Sala tab is one tap away). The room's content is centred under it, up to 100rem.
- **Rooms** (`.sala-rooms`, `.club-room`): the room you are in is gold with a lit edge (the side of the rail row, the top of the phone tab).
- **Office desk**: two columns from `lg`. Left, the inbox ("Caixa de entrada"): sender, date, one sentence and, when the note asks for something, a gold text link to the room where it is settled; the agents' offers of one day read as one note; six notes, then "Ver as N mensagens". Right, stacked panes: table (full club names), last matches, next fixtures, the season's money.
- **Figures**: money is whole reais everywhere (`format.ts`), never ",00". **Dressing room** (`career/Squad.tsx`): a tactics board (`.sala-board`, mown pitch from above with chalk lines) holds eleven magnets (`.sala-magnet`: a badge in the club's colour with the player's rating for that role, surname under it); the squad is a two-column list on a slab. Tap a slot (it turns gold), the list re-sorts by rating for that role, tap the player. The squad is a table: position chip (gold for the eleven, with a hidden "Titular" for readers), name, age (from `sm`), freshness (bar from `sm`, number always; chalk, gold or red by state) and rating; a heavier rule separates the bench.
- **Training ground** (`career/Training.tsx`, plate `treino`): the week's intensity as three stacked options that state their effect in numbers (the chosen one gold), the drill as a gold meter, and the squad's freshness for the next match as a two-column list with a thin bar, the number and a worded state chip (Pronto green, Cansado gold, Esgotado red; never colour alone).
- **Market** (`career/Market.tsx`; plate `olheiro`, the scouts' office): the status slab, then bids and open talks (always on screen), then four parts behind one segmented control: Contratar (agents' offers, then the market, fifteen rows before "Ver os N jogadores"), Seu elenco, Olheiros (scouts and other clubs' squads) and Negócios (the clubs' deals). The shut window reads "Janela fechada" as the headline and the calendar as a sentence under it. Slabs in the order the person acts on them. A status slab (window open in gold, shut in red, the cash, and the other side's last answer as one sentence, `aria-live`). Bids received and open talks appear only when there are any: each is a player line, one sentence stating where the talk stands ("O PLC pede R$ 319.000,00. Ele é titular lá: o clube cobra mais. Restam 4 ofertas."), then native number fields and selects with one gold action and outlined alternatives. Below, two columns: the other clubs' players with position chips (a row ends in "Negociar" or in the reason he will not talk), and the person's squad (what each is to the plan, a toggle to list him) over the clubs' own deals, each with the level the buyer believed he had, how good its office is and its six reasons as numbers. A player of another club shows his level and his potential as a band ("34 a 44"), as wide as the club's scouting is poor; a clause, a contract's end and a loan fee sit in the same line of facts. In the squad the three actions (Renovar, Pôr à venda, Ceder) sit on their own line under the player.
- **Player sheet** (2026-11-20, `career/PlayerSheet.tsx`, worker request `player`): a native `dialog` opened from a player's name in Training and in the market's "Seu elenco", and from a small info button beside each squad row (the row itself still picks the player for a slot). Full screen on a phone, a 30rem panel on the right from `lg`; Esc, the backdrop and the close button end it and focus returns to the opener. Header with the position chip, name and what he is to the eleven, then freshness and the attributes as label, thin bar and number in the domain's groups (technical, mental, tactical, physical, goalkeeping only for keepers), then health and contract as a `dl`. Only what the pack and career hold: no goals or assists.
- **Table and market rows**: the office table adds V, E, D from `sm` (hidden on the phone so 360px fits) and, where the division's first phase holds direct promotion or relegation places, a hairline after the last place that goes up and before the first that falls, with the text legend "Sobem N" / "Caem N". A market player is two lines: position, name and level; then club, age and value, which is also the summary of a native `details` "Mais sobre ele" (wage, contract end, potential, clause, agent, scouting); it is open from `lg` and, on a phone, for the first row of the list.
- **Boardroom** (`career/Board.tsx`, plate `diretoria`): the season's goal as one display sentence with today's position under it (red when behind), the confidence as a 6xl number with a worded state and a meter, then the facts that last moved it, each with its signed points, and the ultimatum. **Dismissal** (`Fired`): the tunnel plate, one headline, one sentence, one gold button to start again.
- **Match** (2026-11-20, `FriendlyPage.tsx` `Match`, `Scoreboard`, `Report`; `pitch.ts` `athlete`): the scoreboard is a slab over the pitch (round badges with initials, both names, the score on gold, the minute under it). Players are drawn as people (shoulders wider than the waist, bare thigh and sock, knee and elbow that bend with the stride, neck, hair, the chest's far side in shade; from behind when running up the screen); the ring under the feet tells the side without colour, whole at home and broken away, in the lighter of the kit's two colours. - **Season verdict** (`ClubCareerPage.tsx` `SeasonMoment`): when the season's turn moves the club, a full-screen scene shows once per season (remembered in `localStorage` `dc_club_turn` as career id and season, so a second career sees its own): the glory plate with "Campeão!" or "Subimos!" in gold, or the stadium's street side with "Rebaixado" in chalk; the badge, one sentence, one gold button to the next season.
