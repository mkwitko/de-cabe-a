# Libertadores and Sul-Americana in the Modo Clube

Owner decision (2026-11-24): real clubs with squads, hybrid for now. Boca and River get their real
FM squads (the only South American squads in the owner's FM export). The other foreign clubs get
generated squads at the right strength, to be replaced when a new FM export with the South
American leagues exists.

## Why not put the foreign clubs in `pack.clubs`

69 reads of `pack.clubs` in 35 domain files treat every club as Brazilian: divisions, state
championships (`city.state`, and Peru `PE` clashes with Pernambuco), the AI market, finance and
boards. The foreign clubs therefore live apart, in a career-side table, until each system is
audited.

## Phase 1: playable cups, quick results (this branch)

- `competition/conmebol-data.ts`: the 2026 editions as data. Formats, dates, berths, prize money,
  every foreign club (name, country, city, altitude, strength), with sources.
  - Libertadores: 47 clubs. First stage (6), second (16, 13 byes), third (8), 8 groups of 4,
    R16, QF and SF two legs, single final (28 Nov, Montevideo; extra time, then penalties).
  - Sul-Americana: 56 clubs. First stage (32, single match, penalties), 8 groups of 4, play-offs
    (group runners-up against the Libertadores group 3rds), R16 to SF two legs, single final.
  - Brazil: 7 Libertadores places (Série A 1-5 and the Copa do Brasil champion straight into the
    groups, 6-7 into the 2nd stage) and 6 Sul-Americana places (Série A 8-13). The rule for 2027
    still needs checking.
- The foreign clubs only play quick results (strength vs strength). A Brazilian club's match
  against one uses `quickResult` with the foreign club's strength.
- The career stand-in for the Conmebol clubs (`continentalOf` in season.ts) reads the real
  qualifiers.
- Money: USD prizes converted with `market/currency.ts`.

## Phase 2a: watched matches (done, no pack change)

- `career/foreign-squad.ts`: each foreign club borrows the squad of the Brazilian club at its rank
  in the opening pack (same attributes and ages, new Spanish names, its country's nationality).
  The squads live outside `pack.players`; only the watched match borrows them (`matchPack`).
- Altitude: McSharry (BMJ 2007), half a goal per 1,000 m in quick results; the visitors lose up
  to 15% of their legs in the engine (PARÂMETRO). Only home cities above 1,500 m count.
- A foreign club at home fills 85% of its stadium (PARÂMETRO).
- CONMEBOL prizes are paid stage by stage, group wins as they come.

## Phase 2b: real data (needs the pack on the VM)

- An optional pack field with the FM reputation of each foreign club (strength) and the real
  Boca and River squads. Merge the schema first; the regenerated pack must then be copied to the
  VM (`apps/api/club-packs/`) by hand, or production keeps the generated squads.
- Signing players from foreign clubs.
