## Known limits

- Scope is the game pool (verified players) plus every Career Path answer; the ~92k imported
  players are untouched (Messi, Q615, is an imported T3 and stays so: not in any game today).
- Wikidata caps (P54 + P1350) are missing for some old players (Jairzinho shows 2 Brazil caps, the
  lineups we hold), so a few legends land one tier low through the caps rules; the World Cup
  winner rule usually catches them.
- Borderline current tiers are kept by design: Vinícius Júnior and Luka Modrić stay T2 (range
  T1–T2). Promote by hand if wanted.
- Sitelinks favour players of today's big leagues; the Brazilian rules (caps, grandes, World Cup)
  balance that, and the pre-1970 era cap keeps historians' names out of T1/T2.
